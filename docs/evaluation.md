# LedgerLens Evaluation & Benchmarking

## Metrics Framework
- **Matching Accuracy**: Precision, Recall, F1 against synthetic ground-truth.
- **Exception Detection**: True positive rate on deliberate synthetic exceptions (fee mismatches, missing bank records, duplicate settlements).
- **Throughput**: Records processed per second across deterministic reconciliation and anomaly scoring.
- **AI Investigation Validity**: Adherence to ground truth evidence citations and rejection of unbacked hypotheses.

*(Benchmark scripts and metrics will be populated in Phase 4 & Phase 8)*
