# LedgerLens 5-Minute Judge Demo Flow

1. **Overview & Problem Statement**: Modern merchants reconcile millions across orders, payment gateways, fees, GST, refunds, and bank settlements. Mismatches create revenue leakage.
2. **One-Click Demo Loading**: Click "Load Demo Dataset" (1,000 synthetic records with ground truth).
3. **Deterministic Reconciliation**: Immediate KPI dashboard showing Matched, Exceptions, Human Review, and precision metrics.
4. **High-Confidence AI Investigation**: View a matched/explained fee+GST discrepancy with a complete evidence trail.
5. **Unexplained Exception & Safeguard**: Show how LedgerLens safely flags ambiguous cases as `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED` rather than hallucinating answers.
6. **Evaluation Benchmark**: Inspect real evaluation metrics and ground truth validation.
